1. Who we are

RSAS Ltd ("RSAS", "we", "us") trades as Research Sourced Art Solutions. We buy and sell works of art and provide research on art acquisitions and sales.

RSAS Ltd is registered in place of registration with company number company number. Our registered office is registered office address.

We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about your data at contact email or UK telephone number.

2. The data we collect

WhenWhat we collect
You send an enquiryYour name, email address or phone number, and your message
You ask us to sell a workDetails and photographs of the work, and what you tell us about its history
You buy or sell through usBilling and delivery details, and the records of the transaction
We carry out identity checksIdentity documents, proof of address and, where the law requires it, the source of funds
You join our mailing listYour name, email address and your interests
You visit the websiteBasic technical logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address, date and time

We do not ask for sensitive personal data, such as health information. Please do not send it to us.

3. Why we use your data, and our legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
To reply to your enquiryOur legitimate interest in answering people who contact us
To arrange a viewing, purchase, sale or consignmentTo take steps before a contract, and to perform a contract with you
To verify your identity and keep the records the law requiresLegal obligation, including anti-money-laundering law
To send news about works and artistsYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time
To keep the website secure and workingOur legitimate interest in running a safe website

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for automated decisions or profiling.

4. Marketing

We send marketing emails only if you have asked for them. Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to stop at contact email.

5. Who we share your data with

  • Service providers who work for us under contract: hosting provider, form provider and email provider.
  • Professional advisers, such as solicitors and accountants.
  • Shippers, insurers, framers and conservators, when you buy or sell a work.
  • The other party to a sale, but only the details needed to complete it, and never an owner's identity without their agreement.
  • Regulators, tax authorities and law enforcement, where the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside the UK and the EEA

We aim to keep your data in the UK or the European Economic Area. If a provider stores data elsewhere, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as approved standard contract clauses.

7. How long we keep your data

DataHow long
Enquiries that do not lead to a transactionUp to 12 months
Transaction, provenance and tax recordsAt least 6 years
Identity and due diligence records5 years after the business relationship ends
Mailing list detailsUntil you unsubscribe

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to ask us to correct or delete it, to ask us to restrict how we use it or object to our use of it, to ask for your data in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us at contact email. We reply within one month. There is no charge.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at contact email. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and reply without undue delay.

You can also complain to a regulator:

  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)
  • Gibraltar: the Gibraltar Regulatory Authority (gra.gi)
  • Spain: the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es)

10. Security

We protect your data with access controls, encrypted connections and careful choice of providers. No website can promise complete security. We will tell you and the regulator if a breach puts you at risk.

11. Children

Our website and services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
