Research Disclaimer

RSAS research reports, artist profiles, market notes and Insights articles are provided for information only. They are based on sources we believe to be reliable at the date of preparation, but we cannot guarantee that every source is complete or accurate, and data on private sales in particular is often incomplete.

Our research is historical and analytical. It is not a forecast of future prices, a formal valuation, an authentication opinion, or financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Selection of an artist under our emerging artist criteria is not a recommendation to buy for financial return.

Works of art are not regulated investments. They can be difficult to sell, their value can fall as well as rise, and past results in either market are no guide to future value. Please take independent advice where appropriate before making any purchase.
