1. The short version

This website does not use cookies for advertising, tracking or analytics. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can save on your phone or computer. Similar tools include tracking pixels and local storage. This policy covers all of them.

3. What this website uses

TypeUsed here?Detail
Advertising and tracking cookiesNoNone
Analytics cookiesNoNone
Social media cookiesNoWe link to Instagram and LinkedIn. We do not embed them
Strictly necessary storageOnly if confirmed as neededFor example, to remember that a form was sent, or to protect the form from spam

Our fonts are stored on our own website, so your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

4. Links to other websites

When you follow a link to Instagram, LinkedIn or another website, that website may set its own cookies. Their policies apply there, not ours.

5. If this changes

If we ever add cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this policy first. We will ask for your consent before any such cookie is set, with a clear choice to accept or reject.
