Rental Terms

Draft for solicitor review. Last updated: [date]. These terms apply to business clients only.

Key terms at a glance

Term Summary
Who can rent Businesses only. Rental is not offered to consumers.
Rental term One, two or three years, renewable.
Rental fee 10% of each work's Agreed Sale Price per year, invoiced quarterly in advance, plus VAT where applicable.
Example A £20,000 work rents for £2,000 a year (£500 a quarter) plus VAT.
Insurance RSAS insures every rented work for its Agreed Sale Price for the full term.
Exchanges You may ask to exchange works at any time during the term.
Option to buy Buy any rented work at any time. All rent paid on that work is credited in full against its price.
Ending early Not within the first 12 months. After that, on 90 days' written notice.
Governing law England and Wales.

1. About these terms

1.1 These Rental Terms apply when RSAS Ltd, trading as Research Sourced Art Solutions ("RSAS", "we", "us" or "our"), rents one or more works of art ("Works") to a business client ("you" or "your").

1.2 Rental is offered only to businesses acting in the course of their trade, business or profession. It is not offered to consumers.

1.3 Each rental is made under a written Rental Agreement. Its schedule (the "Schedule") lists the Works, the agreed sale price of each Work (the "Agreed Sale Price"), the address and position where each Work will hang (the "Location"), the rental term (the "Term") and the start date (the "Start Date").

1.4 These Rental Terms and the Rental Agreement form the whole contract between us. If they conflict, the Rental Agreement takes priority.

1.5 A contract is made only when both parties have signed the Rental Agreement and we have completed the customer due diligence described in clause 16.

2. Ownership

2.1 The Works remain the property of RSAS, or of the artist or gallery lending them through RSAS (the "Owner"), at all times. Ownership passes to you only if you buy a Work under clause 10.

2.2 You must not sell, charge, pledge or offer any Work as security, or allow anyone else to do so. The Works do not form part of your assets. If you become insolvent, you must tell any administrator, liquidator or receiver that the Works belong to the Owner.

2.3 We do not take any lien or security over the Works for our own borrowing.

2.4 Copyright and all other intellectual property rights in each Work remain with the artist or the copyright owner. Renting a Work gives you no right to reproduce it, except as set out in clause 14.

3. Term and renewal

3.1 The Term for each Work is one, two or three years from the Start Date, as stated in the Schedule.

3.2 You may renew the rental of any Work for a further one, two or three years by giving us written notice at least 60 days before the Term ends. Renewal is subject to the Owner's agreement. We may review the Agreed Sale Price on renewal and will tell you of any change before you commit.

3.3 If, at the end of the Term, you have neither renewed, bought the Work nor made it available for collection on the agreed date, the rental continues at the same annual rate, charged monthly in advance, until we collect the Work.

4. Rental fee and payment

4.1 The rental fee for each Work is 10% of its Agreed Sale Price per year, plus VAT where applicable.

4.2 We invoice the rental fee quarterly in advance. The first invoice is issued on signing and must be paid before delivery. Later invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer to the account shown on the invoice.

4.3 We will never change our bank details by email alone. If you receive a message asking you to pay a different account, call us on a number you already hold before making any payment.

4.4 Example: a Work with an Agreed Sale Price of £20,000 rents for £2,000 a year, invoiced at £500 a quarter, plus VAT where applicable.

4.5 If you pay late, we may charge interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. If any sum remains unpaid 30 days after its due date, we may end the rental under clause 12.

4.6 Delivery, installation, de-installation, return transport and any framing made specifically for your scheme are charged at cost. We will agree these costs with you in writing before any work is done.

5. Delivery, installation and the Location

5.1 We deliver and install each Work at the Location using specialist fine art handlers and installers.

5.2 You must give our handlers safe and timely access, provide walls and surfaces suitable for the agreed fixings, and tell us in advance of any hazard, building work or restriction on site.

5.3 Works in public or high-traffic areas are hung with security fixings and, where we advise it, UV-protective glazing. You must not remove or alter these.

6. Looking after the Works

6.1 You must keep each Work at its Location and in its agreed position. You must not move, lend, take off site, alter, clean (other than as set out in clause 6.3), reframe or restore any Work without our prior written consent.

6.2 You must take reasonable care of the Works and follow the care notes we provide. In particular, you must keep them away from direct sunlight, radiators, heaters, air vents, and sources of moisture or steam, and must not hang them where they are likely to be knocked or splashed.

6.3 Housekeeping staff may only dust frames and glazing with a soft, dry cloth. No sprays, liquids or cleaning products may be used on or near a Work.

6.4 You must tell us in writing within 24 hours if any Work is lost, stolen, damaged or shows any change in condition. You must report any theft to the police straight away and give us the crime reference number.

6.5 Only a conservator appointed by us may repair a damaged Work.

7. Insurance

7.1 We insure each Work on an all-risks basis for its Agreed Sale Price, subject to the usual exclusions in a fine art policy, from the time it leaves our handlers' premises for delivery until it is returned to them. The cost of this insurance is included in the rental fee.

7.2 You must not do anything, or allow anything to be done, that could invalidate our insurance. You must co-operate fully with us and our insurers on any claim. A summary of the cover is available on request.

8. Your responsibility for loss or damage

8.1 You are not responsible for fair wear and tear.

8.2 Where our insurers pay a claim for loss of or damage to a Work while it is in your care, your liability is limited to the policy excess, up to a maximum of £[500] for each incident.

8.3 You are fully responsible for loss of or damage to a Work, to the extent our insurers decline or reduce a claim because of your breach of these terms, or because of the negligence or deliberate act of you, your staff or your contractors. In that case you must pay the reasonable cost of conservation and any reduction in the Work's value, or, if the Work is lost or beyond repair, its Agreed Sale Price.

9. Condition reports, inspection, exchanges and recall

9.1 We prepare a written and photographic condition report for each Work on installation and on collection, and send you a copy. If you do not comment on a report within 7 days of receiving it, it will be treated as accepted.

9.2 We, the Owner and our insurers may inspect the Works on reasonable notice, normally at least 2 working days, during your normal business hours. In an emergency we may ask for access sooner.

9.3 You may ask to exchange any Work at any time during the Term. Exchanges depend on availability. The rental fee is adjusted from the exchange date to reflect the Agreed Sale Price of the replacement Work. Transport and installation for an exchange are charged at cost. An exchange does not change the date on which the Term ends.

9.4 Rent paid on a Work you return in an exchange is credited against that Work only, if you later buy it during the same Term. It does not transfer to the replacement Work.

9.5 If the Owner of a lent Work needs it back, for example for an exhibition or a sale, we will give you at least 30 days' written notice. Before the Work is recalled, we will offer you the chance to buy it under clause 10. If you do not buy it, we will replace it with a Work of comparable value and character, at no transport or installation cost to you, or refund any rental fee paid for the period after collection.

10. Your option to buy

10.1 You may buy any rented Work at any time during the Term, or at its end, for its Agreed Sale Price shown in the Schedule.

10.2 All rental fees you have paid on that Work, excluding VAT, are credited in full against its Agreed Sale Price.

10.3 Example: a Work with an Agreed Sale Price of £20,000, rented for two years, will have earned a credit of £4,000. The balance to pay is £16,000, plus any VAT and Artist's Resale Right that apply to the sale.

10.4 A purchase is made under our Conditions of Sale and is subject to customer due diligence. Ownership passes to you only when we have received full payment in cleared funds. From that moment our insurance ends and you become responsible for insuring the Work.

11. Ending the rental early

11.1 You may not end the rental of a Work during the first 12 months of its Term, except by paying the rental fee due for the rest of those 12 months.

11.2 After the first 12 months, you may end the rental of any or all Works by giving us at least 90 days' written notice.

11.3 When a rental ends early, rental fees for the period up to the end date are not refundable. We will refund, pro rata, any fee paid in advance for a period after the end date. De-installation and return transport are charged at cost.

12. When we may end the rental

12 We may end the rental of some or all Works immediately by written notice if:

(a) any sum remains unpaid 30 days after its due date and we have sent you a reminder;

(b) you commit a serious breach of these terms which, if it can be put right, you have not put right within 14 days of our written notice;

(c) you become insolvent, enter into any arrangement with creditors, or stop trading at the Location;

(d) we reasonably believe that a Work is at risk of loss or damage; or

(e) we are unable to complete, or must stop, our customer due diligence, or continuing would breach any law or sanctions regime.

12.1 If we end the rental, you must make the Works available for collection straight away and pay all sums due, including our reasonable collection costs.

13. Return of the Works

13.1 At the end of the rental, you must make each Work available for collection on the agreed date, in the same condition as recorded on installation, apart from fair wear and tear.

13.2 Only our handlers may de-install the Works. You must not take them down or pack them yourself.

14. Photographs and publicity

14.1 You may photograph the Works as they hang at the Location for your own marketing, website, social media and press, provided you credit the artist and title where reasonably practicable.

14.2 You must not reproduce any Work on merchandise, prints, packaging or advertising in which the Work is the main subject without a written licence from the copyright owner, which we can help arrange. You must respect the artist's moral rights, including the right not to have the Work shown in a distorted or altered form.

15. Our liability

15.1 Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

15.2 Subject to clause 15.1, our total liability to you under or in connection with any rental is limited to the rental fees you have paid in the 12 months before the claim arose. We are not liable for loss of profit, revenue, business or goodwill, or for any indirect or consequential loss.

16. Due diligence and personal data

16.1 Before any rental starts, we carry out customer due diligence, which may include checks on your business, its beneficial owners and the source of funds, together with sanctions screening.

16.2 We handle personal data as described in our Privacy Policy.

17. Events outside our control

17.1 Neither of us is responsible for a delay or failure caused by events outside our reasonable control. Payment obligations are not suspended by such events. If an event prevents the rental continuing for more than 60 days, either of us may end it by written notice.

18. General

18.1 Notices must be in writing and sent by email to gallery@rsasltd.com, or to the email or postal address in the Rental Agreement for you.

18.2 You may not transfer your rights or obligations without our written consent. We may transfer our rights to the Owner of a Work.

18.3 Any change to these terms or to a Rental Agreement must be agreed in writing by both of us.

18.4 If any part of these terms is found to be unenforceable, the rest remains in force.

18.5 The Owner of a lent Work may enforce clauses 2, 6 and 13 under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999. No one else has any right to enforce these terms.

19. Law and courts

19.1 These terms and any rental are governed by the law of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
