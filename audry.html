Editions

Each work is a limited edition of 8, plus 2 artist's proofs. Every edition is individually numbered and comes with an Artist Certificate of Authenticity signed by AUDRY., and a Production Certificate recording how and where it was made.

  • Pigment print on Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308 gsm, 100% cotton paper
  • Mounted on aluminium Dibond, matte finish
  • Frameless, or with optional museum-quality framing
  • Also available as museum acrylic
  • Custom and institutional sizes on request
