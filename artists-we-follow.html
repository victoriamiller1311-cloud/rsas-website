Artists We Follow

The contemporary artists on whom RSAS keeps active research.

This is not a list of recommendations, and it is not a stock list. Some of these artists we have bought for clients. Others we follow closely without having bought at all. A few we have studied in depth and concluded that their market does not yet justify the prices being asked.

Each profile shows which market an artist trades in, so you can see at a glance how much evidence there is to go on.

Christian Hook

Artist
Gibraltarian, born 1971
Lives and works
Gibraltar and London
Career stage
Established
Representation
Clarendon Fine Art

Painter known for portraits and horses, built up in transparent layers to show motion and time. Studied illustration at Middlesex University. Sky Arts Portrait Artist of the Year 2014, with a portrait of Alan Cumming in the Scottish National Portrait Gallery.
