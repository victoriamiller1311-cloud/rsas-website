Artists

The living artists whose work RSAS handles, follows and researches. Works from private family collections are on Current Works.

This is not a list of recommendations, and it is not a stock list. Some of these artists we have bought for clients.

Others we follow closely without having bought at all. A few we have studied in depth and concluded that their market does not yet justify the prices being asked.

Each profile shows which market an artist trades in, so you can see at a glance how much evidence there is to go on.
