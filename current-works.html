Current Works

Contemporary and emerging art, alongside selected blue-chip works, modern masters and established artists’ prints.

Our selection spans contemporary and emerging artists, blue-chip and modern masters, including Marc Chagall, and selected prints by established artists. Works are offered from our own inventory or on behalf of galleries, artists and private owners. Every one has passed through our research and verification process, and a research summary is available on request.

What you see here is only part of the picture. Many of the works we place are never listed, because they are sourced privately against a client's brief.
