RSAS buys and sells contemporary art for private collectors, businesses and design-led spaces. Before any work changes hands, our researchers establish what the artist's market really looks like: what galleries charge, what collectors have paid on resale, and where the evidence stops. You read that research before you commit to anything.

What We Do

Buying

We find the work, check it thoroughly and negotiate the price. Much of what we place never reaches a public sale, because galleries and studios offer their best pieces first to people they trust.

Selling

Price, channel and timing, decided on evidence rather than optimism. Then we run the sale for you, wherever the work was first bought.

Research

A written account of an artist's market, available on its own and with no obligation to buy. Everything else we do is built on it.

Corporate and design

Collections for hotels, workplaces and design projects, bought outright or rented on terms of one, two or three years.

Private Collections

We also sell privately owned works on behalf of their owners, including works from family collections. Each is offered with the owner’s written authority, and its provenance is researched before it is listed.

RSAS is independent of the artists, estates and foundations whose work it handles.

Selling Through RSAS

Research Comes First

The art market runs on information that is hard to come by. Prices are agreed in private, results are published selectively, and the person selling a work is usually the one explaining what it is worth. Few buyers would accept that arrangement for any other purchase of similar value.

We start from the other end. Our researchers establish the facts before any purchase or sale is discussed, and their findings are never adjusted to help a deal along. If the evidence does not support a price, we say so. That applies to the works we own as well.

How Our Research Works

Emerging Artists, Properly Vetted

Some of the most rewarding works in any collection are bought early, before an artist is widely known. It is also where the market is least forgiving. Every emerging artist we put forward has been tested against nine published criteria, checked by a second researcher and, wherever possible, visited in their studio. Most of the artists we look at do not make it through.
